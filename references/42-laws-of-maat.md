# The 42 Laws of Maat

The 42 declarations of ethical conduct (the "negative confessions") from the
Papyrus of Ani, Book of the Dead, Chapter 125. Recited by the deceased before
Osiris and the 42 judges, prior to the weighing of the heart against the
feather of Maat.

Maat is truth, balance, order, law, morality, and justice — the oldest attested
African ethical-legal concept, and the namesake of this tool. These declarations
are the ethical core the House of Maat stands on: law in service of justice,
not the other way around.

Translation: E.A. Wallis Budge (public domain). Each declaration is addressed
to one of the 42 judges, named in parentheses.

1. I have not committed sin. (Hail, Usekh-nemmt, who comest forth from Anu)
2. I have not committed robbery with violence. (Hail, Hept-khet, who comest forth from Kher-aha)
3. I have not stolen. (Hail, Fenti, who comest forth from Khemenu)
4. I have not slain men and women. (Hail, Am-khaibit, who comest forth from Qerrt)
5. I have not stolen grain. (Hail, Neha-her, who comest forth from Rasta)
6. I have not purloined offerings. (Hail, Ruruti, who comest forth from heaven)
7. I have not stolen the property of the gods. (Hail, Arfi-em-khet, who comest forth from Suat)
8. I have not uttered lies. (Hail, Neba, who comest forth and retreatest)
9. I have not carried away food. (Hail, Set-qesu, who comest forth from Hensu)
10. I have not uttered curses. (Hail, Utu-nesert, who comest forth from Het-ka-Ptah)
11. I have not committed adultery, I have not lain with men. (Hail, Qerrti, who comest forth from Amentet)
12. I have made none to weep. (Hail, Her-f-ha-f, who comest forth from Tep-ahet)
13. I have not eaten the heart. (Hail, Basti, who comest forth from Bast)
14. I have not attacked any man. (Hail, Ta-retiu, who comest forth from the night)
15. I am not a man of deceit. (Hail, Unem-snef, who comest forth from the execution chamber)
16. I have not stolen cultivated land. (Hail, Unem-besek, who comest forth from Mabit)
17. I have not been an eavesdropper. (Hail, Neb-maat, who comest forth and retreatest)
18. I have not slandered [no man]. (Hail, Tenemiu, who comest forth from Bast)
19. I have not been angry without just cause. (Hail, Sertiu, who comest forth from Anu)
20. I have not debauched the wife of any man. (Hail, Tutu, who comest forth from Ati)
21. I have not debauched the wife of [any] man. (Hail, Uamenti, who comest forth from the Khebt chamber)
22. I have not polluted myself. (Hail, Maa-antuf, who comest forth from Per-Menu)
23. I have terrorized none. (Hail, Heri-uru, who comest forth from Tchatchat)
24. I have not transgressed [the law]. (Hail, Khemiu, who comest forth from Khemenu)
25. I have not been wroth. (Hail, Shet-kheru, who comest forth from Urit)
26. I have not shut my ears to the words of truth. (Hail, Nekhenu, who comest forth from Heqat)
27. I have not blasphemed. (Hail, Kenemti, who comest forth from Kenmet)
28. I am not a man of violence. (Hail, An-hetep-f, who comest forth from Sau)
29. I am not a stirrer up of strife. (Hail, Sera-kheru, who comest forth from Unaset)
30. I have not acted with undue haste. (Hail, Neb-heru, who comest forth from Netchfet)
31. I have not pried into matters. (Hail, Sekhriu, who comest forth from Uthen)
32. I have not multiplied my words in speaking. (Hail, Neb-abui, who comest forth from Sauti)
33. I have wronged none, I have done no evil. (Hail, Nefer-Tem, who comest forth from Het-ka-Ptah)
34. I have not worked witchcraft against the king. (Hail, Tem-Sepu, who comest forth from Tetu)
35. I have never stopped [the flow of] water. (Hail, Ari-em-ab-f, who comest forth from Tebti)
36. I have never raised my voice. (Hail, Ahi, who comest forth from Nu)
37. I have not cursed God. (Hail, Uatch-rekhit, who comest forth from Sau)
38. I have not acted with arrogance. (Hail, Neheb-nefert, who comest forth from Het-ka-Ptah)
39. I have not stolen the bread of the gods. (Hail, Neheb-kau, who comest forth from thy city)
40. I have not carried away the khenfu cakes from the spirits of the dead. (Hail, Tcheser-tep, who comest forth from the shrine)
41. I have not snatched away the bread of the child, nor treated with contempt the god of my city. (Hail, An-af, who comest forth from Maati)
42. I have not slain the cattle belonging to the god. (Hail, Hetch-abhu, who comest forth from Ta-she)
